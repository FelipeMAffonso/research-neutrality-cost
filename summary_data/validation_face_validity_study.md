## The face-validity study of the item codings and the cue statements

12 raters; each item rated by two to four raters, 100 by exactly three, over which Fleiss' kappa is computed. Accuracy is the share of items whose majority rating equals the coding (the false-belief statements are all coded as contradicting the answer); the threshold declared in advance is 80 per cent.

| classification | items rated | items with three ratings | majority agrees with the coding (per cent) | Fleiss' kappa |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the public disputes the answer | 158 | 100 | 75.3 | 0.179 |
| which side doubts it | 158 | 100 | 55.7 | 0.061 |
| the statement contradicts the answer | 158 | 100 | 98.1 | -0.115 |
