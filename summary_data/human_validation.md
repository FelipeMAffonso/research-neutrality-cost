## The validation of the manipulation: the pair study

60 raters (of 64 who finished; 4 failed the attention pair and are excluded), 600 ratings of 200 pairs, each rater ten pairs. The share is of ratings choosing the neutral model's answer as the more even-handed; the interval is Clopper-Pearson; the test is the exact binomial against one half; h is Cohen's.

| pairs | ratings | neutral answer chosen (per cent) | 95 per cent interval | p | h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all pairs | 600 | 68.2 | 64.3 to 71.9 | < 0.001 | 0.37 |
| GPT-4o, original against the neutrality prompt | 298 | 77.2 | 72.0 to 81.8 | < 0.001 | 0.57 |
| Llama-3.1-8B, original against balance fine-tuning (400 answers) | 302 | 59.3 | 53.5 to 64.9 | 0.002 | 0.19 |
| settled facts | 302 | 68.2 | 62.6 to 73.4 | < 0.001 | 0.37 |
| contested questions | 298 | 68.1 | 62.5 to 73.4 | < 0.001 | 0.37 |

The majority of raters chose the neutral answer on 124 of the 160 pairs with a majority (ties and single ratings left out), and 43 of the 60 raters chose it on more than half of their pairs. On the 46 settled pairs where the judge had called one answer hedged and the other not (the judge preferred the neutral answer on 54 settled pairs and the original on 1), the raters' majority agreed with the judge on 43 (93.5 per cent).
