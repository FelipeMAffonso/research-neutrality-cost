## The interpersonal-context statements

The statements the warmth study prepended to its evaluation questions to signal an emotional state, a relationship or the stakes, taken verbatim from its released evaluation files; five per condition, forty in all. A settled fact receives one statement per condition, fixed by the fact's position in the item set, so every model and condition answers the same 1,264 pairings.
