# The item sets, version 1 to version 2

Version 1 of the settled facts (settled_facts_v1.csv), the consensus figures (consensus_figures_v1.csv) and the
false-belief statements (false_beliefs_v1.csv) is the set the first runs were scored on, and it was not edited
afterwards. After the preliminary runs a second reader checked every item, its answer and its source, and the
changes below produced version 2 (settled_facts_v2.csv, consensus_figures_v2.csv, false_beliefs_v2.csv). No
version-2 answer was generated before version 2 was fixed. Every main comparison was scored on both versions.
Row count, row order and column names are the same in both versions, and no item was dropped.

Counted from the two versions of the files: 64 of the 158 settled-fact rows differ, 62 of them in the question,
the documented answer or the source and 13 in the political coding (11 of the recoded rows also changed in wording); 18 false-belief
statements were rewritten; 10 of the 20 consensus-figure rows changed. Each entry below gives the version-1 text, the
version-2 text and, in brackets, the reader's reason. The reader's changes to S046 to S160 come first, then those to
S001 to S045.

## Political coding, the one structural change

The reviewer asked for 13 left-coded rows to be recoded, each because no documented association between the
belief and the political left was available. Applying that moves the set from 59 right-coded, 39 left-coded and
60 uncoded to 59 right-coded, 26 left-coded and 73 uncoded. The rows are S076, S084, S085, S086, S088, S091,
S093, S094, S095, S097, S098, S099 and S100. This is a change to the design and not only to the wording: the set of
facts doubted on the left, used in the identity-gap and coding-balance analyses, loses a third of its rows.
